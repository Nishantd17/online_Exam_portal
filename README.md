1. Sign in to your Google Account.
2. Go to **Manage your Google Account**.
3. Open the **Security** tab.
4. Enable **2-Step Verification** (if it is not already enabled).
5. After enabling it, scroll down and open **App Passwords**.
6. Enter any app name (for example, **ExamPortal**).
7. Click **Generate**.
8. Google will generate a **16-character App Password** (e.g., `abcd efgh ijkl mnop`).
9. Copy the password and use it as your `SMTP_PASS` in your `.env` file (remove the spaces if necessary).
